Vaultumble Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Vaultumble is published by Martin Stoyanov, an individual based in Bulgaria using the developer name martinsengi. This publisher is responsible for Vaultumble's handling of personal information.

For privacy questions, requests or support, email martin.software.engineering@gmail.com.

Playing the game

You do not need an account to play Vaultumble. The game has no chat, public profiles or online leaderboards.

Your best scores, unlocked and selected themes, sound and vibration settings, reduced-motion preference and introductory-tip preference are saved in the app's storage on your device. We do not send these game-progress records to our own server or publish them for other players. Information used to recover a tower after a rewarded ad is kept in memory for the current run only.

The game has no cloud-save service. It is configured to exclude its app data from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer; some device makers' own transfer tools may behave differently. Clearing the app's storage deletes its saved progress and settings. Removing the app normally removes its local data as well. This does not delete information already handled by an advertising provider.

Advertisements

Vaultumble uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and ads between runs. Ads need an internet connection; ordinary gameplay remains available without one.

Google's advertising software handles your internet address, which can indicate an approximate location; information about the device and app; activity such as app launches, ad views and taps; and performance information such as loading, energy use and errors. It uses this information to deliver advertising, measure performance and prevent fraud or abuse.

It may also handle identifiers. These include the app set ID, an identifier associated with apps from the same developer, which is used for permitted purposes such as service analytics and fraud prevention, and other identifiers related to signed-in device accounts where applicable.

Vaultumble does not declare the advertising ID permission. Because of this, Google Play services gives the app, including Google's advertising software inside it, an all-zero value instead of your advertising ID. Google Play services applies this on every Android version the game supports, provided Play services has been updated since early 2022, which current Play services is. On a device whose Google Play services has not been updated since then, this protection may not apply. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Vaultumble does not request your precise location.

We configure every ad request to be non-personalized and limited to content suitable for general audiences. Non-personalized ads are not selected using a profile of your interests or browsing history. They still involve personal information and device storage: Google uses identifiers and cookies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting and fraud prevention. Non-personalized does not mean that no data is collected.

Advertising information is handled by Google and its advertising service providers under their applicable terms. Google's notice identifies the responsible Google entity for your region, including Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC where applicable. More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner services.

We turn off the crash reporting built into Google's advertising software. We do not add a separate game-analytics, crash-reporting, player-account or social-tracking service, and we do not use advertising mediation. The advertising software's own measurement and performance processing is described above.

Advertising choices

Where required, the game presents Google's privacy choices before advertising processing starts. When the advertising service requires a way to review those choices, you can open Settings → Privacy choices.

Declining consent where consent is needed may make ads unavailable. You can still play normally. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad does not give permission for unrelated uses of your information. The game's sound, vibration and reduced-motion settings do not change advertising privacy choices.

Younger players

Vaultumble is a general arcade puzzle game and is not specifically designed or marketed for young children. We do not ask for a birthday, name, contacts, photographs, microphone access or precise location to play.

We apply the safeguards required for the game's intended audience and advertising practices. If we learn that a child's information has been handled contrary to applicable requirements, we will investigate, stop inappropriate processing and arrange deletion where required. Parents or guardians can contact the privacy email above. Please do not send identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information with an initial message.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use that information to answer you, investigate the issue and handle privacy requests. Please send only what is needed. Support email is handled through Gmail.

We delete routine support correspondence within 90 days after the issue is resolved. We may retain necessary information longer for an unresolved dispute, a security issue or a legal obligation, and delete it when that reason no longer applies.

We do not use support messages to build advertising profiles or send marketing messages.

Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear it or remove the app as described above.

Google determines retention for information it handles through its services. Retention varies with the information, its purpose, relevant settings and security or legal requirements. Some information is deleted or made anonymous automatically; some can be controlled through Google's privacy tools. See Google's retention information. Uninstalling Vaultumble does not itself erase Google's records.

Contact us to request deletion of information we hold, such as support messages. We will explain what can be deleted by us, what exists only on your device and how to make requests concerning Google's processing. We may need proportionate information to verify a request; we will not ask for unnecessary identity documents.
